IELTS deep dive

Every module. Examiner-standard feedback.

Whether you need IELTS for university, immigration, or professional registration — we build the specific skills each module demands.

🎧
Listening
Accent recognition across British, Australian, and North American speakers. Note-taking under time pressure. Section 3 and 4 academic lecture strategies.
📄
Reading
Skimming and scanning techniques. True/False/Not Given — the question type most students lose marks on. Matching headings and sentence completion.
✍️
Writing
Task 1 — graphs, charts, diagrams, and letter writing. Task 2 — structured academic essays with coherence and lexical resource feedback aligned to IELTS band descriptors.
🗣️
Speaking
Part 1 fluency, Part 2 long-turn preparation, Part 3 discussion. Pronunciation, grammar range, and natural pacing — not scripted answers.
